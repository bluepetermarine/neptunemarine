Type & category
Type IX Powered boats fitted with a buoyancy tube covering at least 85% of the port and starboard sides, suitable for inshore and sheltered waters up to and including wind force 6 Beaufort and significant wave heights up to 2 m (design categories C and D), with a hull length between 8 m and 24 m and a motor power rating of 15 kW and greater.
Type X Powered boats fitted with a buoyancy tube covering at least 85% of the port and starboard sides, suitable for navigation up to wind force 8 Beaufort and significant wave heights up to 4 m (design category B), with a hull length between 8 m and 24 m and a motor power rating of 75 kW and greater.
Design category B — Notified Body required. Declaring this boat as category B means it cannot be assessed by internal production control alone. Under the Recreational Craft Directive 2013/53/EU the stability and design-and-construction assessment must involve a Notified Body. A Type X hull may instead be declared category C or D, which avoids this. Stability is assessed to the latest ISO 12217-1.

Formula inputs. The material-test formulas take d as the maximum tube diameter in millimetres and p as the nominal pressure in bar. Tear resistance Ft takes the greater of the calculated value and the 75 N floor (5.2.2.5); seam strength Fs is ten times Ft. Symbols used in the data fields
LH Hull length — length of the rigid hull, measured to ISO 8666 with the tubes at normal pressure. m
Lmax Maximum length — overall length including anything that projects, tubes at normal pressure. m
Bmax Maximum beam — the widest point, tubes at normal pressure. m
mLC Light craft mass — the finished boat empty of crew, cargo, fuel and stores (light craft condition, ISO 12217-1). kg
mLDC Loaded mass — the boat at its full rated load: crew, stores, fuel, engines, everything (loaded displacement condition, ISO 8666). kg
mT Trailer mass — mass of the boat as it sits on a trailer for towing. Used for the lifting-point design load. kg
Pcalc Calculated motor power — the reference maximum engine power for the boat's size, from length, beam and mass. If the fitted power is higher, the manoeuvring-speed test applies. kW
V Total buoyant volume — the whole volume that keeps the boat afloat: tubes plus any built-in foam, sealed air compartments and buoyant rigid parts. m³
Vc Compartment volume — the volume of one buoyancy-tube air chamber. m³
N Number of separate air compartments in the tube. Minimum five.
LT Total tube length — the buoyancy tube on every side of the boat, added together. m
LSTS Sample tube length — the length of the short off-cut of buoyancy tube used in the attachment strength test. You choose it; minimum 0,5 m. m
mt Test weight mass — the weight dropped in the attachment test, worked out from LSTS, LT and mLDC. kg
d Tube diameter — the largest diameter of the buoyancy tube, measured on the straight sections. mm
p Nominal pressure — the normal working pressure of the tube at 20 °C. bar
Ft Tear resistance — the minimum tearing force the tube fabric must withstand. N
Fs Seam strength — the minimum load a tube seam must hold. N
ALV Windage area — the side-on area of the hull the wind pushes against, at the loading assessed. Used in the stability check. m²
Key terms
RIB Rigid inflatable boat — a rigid lower hull with a soft buoyancy tube (inflated or foam-filled) along the sides, where the tube gives at least half of the buoyancy the boat needs.
Inflatable tube A buoyancy tube split into several separate air chambers, inflated with air, running along both sides.
Foam-filled tube A buoyancy tube filled with closed-cell foam instead of air.
Crew limit (CL) The largest number of people allowed on board when underway, as shown on the builder's plate.
Nominal pressure The correct everyday operating pressure for the tubes at 20 °C.
Significant wave height The average height of the highest third of the waves — the standard way sea state is described.
Category B — offshore Suited to seas up to 4 m wave height and wind up to Beaufort 8.
Category C — inshore Suited to seas up to 2 m and wind up to Beaufort 6.
Category D — sheltered Suited to waves up to 0,3 m (occasional 0,5 m) and wind up to Beaufort 4.
Type IX RIB with tube covering at least 85% of each side, categories C and D, hull 8–24 m, power 15 kW and greater.
Type X RIB with tube covering at least 85% of each side, category B, hull 8–24 m, power 75 kW and greater.
Builder's plate The permanent plate on the boat showing category, crew limit, maximum load and tube pressures.
WIN Watercraft Identification Number — the boat's unique identifying number.
Type test A one-off test on a representative boat or sample that qualifies the model, not repeated on every unit built.
